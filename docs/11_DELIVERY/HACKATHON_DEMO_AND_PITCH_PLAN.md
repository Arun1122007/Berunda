## 1. Demo Script (5 Minutes)

### 0:00-0:30 — Opening

**Presenter:** Narrator

"Project Berunda is an AI-assisted investigative intelligence platform that sits on top of the Karnataka Police FIR database. It helps investigators find hidden links between cases, people, and vehicles — turning isolated FIRs into a connected intelligence graph."

### 0:30-1:30 — Import + Entity Resolution

**Live screen:** Dashboard → Import 4 FIRs → Search "Venkatesh"

"Here I'm importing FIRs. Each has a narrative — a BriefFacts — and NER extracts persons, vehicles, and locations automatically. Now let me search for 'Venkatesh.' I see one PersonEntity linked to 4 cases. In the original data, this person appears as Venkatesh, Venkatesha, Venkat, and V. Entity resolution handled the phonetic variations and proposed the match with 82% confidence, which I confirmed."

### 1:30-2:00 — Relationship Graph

**Live screen:** Click PersonEntity → Relationship Graph tab

"Now I can see Venkatesh's network — co-accused across cases, accused-victim relationships. This is computed in real time by our NetworkX engine. Let me find a hidden link: the shortest path between Case 001 and Case 042 goes through Venkatesh. Without our platform, this connection would be invisible."

### 2:00-2:30 — Geospatial Hotspots + Anomalies

**Live screen:** Switch to Hotspot Map → Drill down → Click anomaly

"Our hotspot map shows crime density by district. I can drill down from state to district to station. Our anomaly detection shows that Bengaluru Urban has a z-score of 4.2 this week — 5 times the normal rate of theft. This is flagged for analyst review."

### 2:30-3:00 — Risk Scoring

**Live screen:** Open Risk Explorer → Select high-risk person → Show feature importance

"Berunda computes a repeat-offender risk score for every resolved person using QuickML AutoML. Here's a person with 5 prior cases — the model assigns an 85% risk score and shows why: prior case count, crime diversity, and recency are the top factors. Caste and religion are hard-excluded from all models."

### 3:00-3:30 — "Ask Berunda" RAG

**Live screen:** Open "Ask Berunda" chat → Type: "What is the connection between FIR-001 and FIR-042?"

"The Ask Berunda feature lets investigators ask plain-English questions. 'What is the connection between FIR-001 and FIR-042?' The answer cites both case numbers and explains that Venkatesh appears as accused in both. Every answer is grounded in retrieved case records."

### 3:30-4:00 — Governance

**Live screen:** Show Fairness Dashboard → Show Audit Log

"And finally, governance: our fairness check dashboard confirms that all models pass the caste/religion exclusion test — every day, automated. Our audit log records every sensitive read, every AI output, every merge decision. This is an append-only, queryable trail."

### 4:00-4:30 — Closing

**Presenter:** Narrator

"Built on Catalyst by Zoho. 5,000 synthetic FIRs. 12 features. All AI is advisory, explainable, and auditable. Thank you."

## 4. Q&A Preparation

| Likely Question | Answer |
|----------------|--------|
| "Is this deployed on Catalyst?" | Yes — Catalyst Functions, AppSail, QuickML, Data Store. Full compliance. |
| "How is this different from CCTNS?" | CCTNS is a system of record. Berunda is an intelligence layer on top — entity resolution, graph analytics, risk scoring. |
| "What about Kannada?" | Phase 1 is English. Kannada NER is our stretch goal and part of the Phase 2 roadmap. |
| "Is the data real?" | No — all data is synthetic, generated by Faker, seeded for reproducibility. Every screen says 'SYNTHETIC DATA.' |
| "How do you handle caste/religion?" | Both are hard-excluded from all models. Compliance role can access them for statutory reporting only. Our fairness check verifies this every day. |
| "What about scale?" | 5,000 FIRs in the demo. Architecture targets 100K+ with event-driven and Neo4j in Phase 3. |
