## 2. Recommended Demo Walkthrough Flow

1. **Access Web App:** Open the live Web Client link above.
2. **Dashboard Overview:** View top KPI metrics (My Assigned Cases, Pending Reviews, Unassigned Cases, Total in Station).
3. **FIR Management:** Navigate to `FIR Cases` to view, filter, and inspect case details across 40,823 synthetic records.
4. **Link Graph:** Click `Link Graph` to explore entity-to-entity crime network visualizations powered by Cytoscape.js.
5. **Hotspot Map:** Click `Hotspot Map` to observe spatial crime clusters.
6. **Analytics & Risk Matrix:** Explore risk scores and statistical anomalies across police stations.
7. **Ask Berunda (RAG Search):** Execute natural-language case queries over the FIR corpus.
