OLAP Database

Execute complex analytical queries with Catalyst's proprietary OLAP (Online Analytical Processing) database. The read-optimized OLAP DB is designed for advanced data analysis and business intelligence. Simply enable the OLAP DB to initiate a sync with your primary relational DB, and execute your ZCQL queries directly on it instead!

Key Features

Advanced Analytical Query Executions

Execute advanced ZCQL queries with analytical functions on the OLAP database from the console, or through various SDKs. Catalyst delivers a scalable, streamlined, and hassle-free experience.

Data Analysis with OLAP Operations

Perform intricate analysis on multidimensional data and make informed business decisions. Drill-down details, roll-up data to aggregate, or slice it and dice it to obtain subsets.

Platform-Managed Storage

Catalyst eliminates any efforts from your end in setting up or managing the OLAP DB, and simplifies its usage significantly.

Real-Time Sync with Primary DB

Data from your relational DB is automatically synced with the OLAP DB in real-time, allowing you to perform upto-date analysis.

Instant Results with Large Datasets

Experience instant query executions on datasets of any scale, without timeouts or lags even with the largest of datasets.
